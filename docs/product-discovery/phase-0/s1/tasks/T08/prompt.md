Feature: `slugify(text, { maxLength })`. When `maxLength` is given, the slug must be at most that many characters, cut at a word (hyphen) boundary when possible, and never end with a hyphen. If the first word alone is longer than `maxLength`, cut that word at `maxLength`. Example: `slugify("The quick brown fox", { maxLength: 12 })` → `"the-quick"`. Without the option, behaviour is unchanged.
